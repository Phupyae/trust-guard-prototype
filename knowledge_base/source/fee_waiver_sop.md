# Customer Fee Waiver Authority SOP

## 1 Purpose
This standard operating procedure sets out who at Northbridge Bank (a fictional organisation) may waive fees and charges on individual customer accounts, and how waivers are recorded.

## 2 Scope
This SOP applies to service fees and charges on individual customer accounts, such as fall-below fees, card replacement fees and late payment charges. It does not apply to interest charges or to fees on corporate accounts.

## 3 Waiver Authority
### 3.1 Customer Service Officer
A Customer Service Officer may waive fees of up to $50 per customer in any 12-month period.
### 3.2 Branch Manager
Waivers above $50 and up to $500 per customer in any 12-month period require Branch Manager approval.
### 3.3 Regional Head
Waivers above $500 per customer in any 12-month period require approval from the Regional Head of Branch Banking.

## 4 Restrictions
### 4.1 Late Payment Charges
Credit card late payment charges may be waived at most 2 times per customer in any 12-month period.
### 4.2 Non-Waivable Charges
Charges collected on behalf of government agencies, such as stamp duty, must not be waived.

## 5 Recording Waivers
Every waiver must be recorded in the Customer Relationship Management system with a reason code on the same business day.
