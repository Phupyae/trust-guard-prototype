# Branch Over-the-Counter Cash Withdrawal SOP

## 1 Purpose
This standard operating procedure describes how branch staff of Northbridge Bank (a fictional organisation) process over-the-counter cash withdrawals by individual customers.

## 2 Scope
This SOP applies to over-the-counter SGD cash withdrawals from individual savings and current accounts at any branch. It does not apply to ATM withdrawals or corporate accounts.

## 3 Approval Limits
### 3.1 Withdrawals up to $30,000
Withdrawals of up to $30,000 may be processed by the teller after identity verification. No further approval is required.
### 3.2 Withdrawals above $30,000
Withdrawals above $30,000 require Branch Manager approval. The customer may collect the cash on the same day.

## 4 Identity Verification
The teller must verify the customer's original NRIC or passport before processing any withdrawal.
