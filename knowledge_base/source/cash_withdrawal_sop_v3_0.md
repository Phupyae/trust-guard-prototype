# Branch Over-the-Counter Cash Withdrawal SOP

## 1 Purpose
This standard operating procedure describes how branch staff of Northbridge Bank (a fictional organisation) process over-the-counter cash withdrawals by individual customers. Version 3.0 replaces version 2.1 and lowers the approval thresholds.

## 2 Scope
This SOP applies to over-the-counter SGD cash withdrawals from individual savings and current accounts at any branch. It does not apply to ATM withdrawals, corporate accounts or foreign currency withdrawals.

## 3 Approval Limits
### 3.1 Withdrawals up to $20,000
Withdrawals of up to $20,000 may be processed by the teller after identity verification. No further approval is required.
### 3.2 Withdrawals above $20,000 up to $50,000
Withdrawals above $20,000 and up to $50,000 require Branch Manager approval. The customer may collect the cash on the same day.
### 3.3 Withdrawals above $50,000
Withdrawals above $50,000 require approval from both the Branch Manager and the Branch Operations Head. The customer must give 1 business day of advance notice so that the branch can arrange the cash.

## 4 Identity and Purpose Checks
### 4.1 Identity Verification
The teller must verify the customer's original NRIC or passport before processing any withdrawal.
### 4.2 Purpose of Withdrawal
For withdrawals above $20,000, the teller must ask for and record the purpose of the withdrawal in the Branch Transaction System.

## 5 Third-Party Withdrawals
A third party may withdraw cash on a customer's behalf only with a signed Letter of Authorisation and the third party's original NRIC or passport. Third-party withdrawals are limited to $5,000 per day.

## 6 Suspicious Transactions
If the teller suspects that a withdrawal is linked to fraud or a scam, the teller must pause the transaction and escalate to the Branch Manager immediately. The Branch Manager must report the case to Group Compliance within 1 business day.
