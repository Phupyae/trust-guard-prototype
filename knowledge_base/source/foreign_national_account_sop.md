# Personal Account Opening for Foreign Nationals SOP

## 1 Purpose
This standard operating procedure describes how branch staff of Northbridge Bank (a fictional organisation) open personal accounts for foreign nationals.

## 2 Scope
This SOP applies to individuals who are neither Singapore Citizens nor Singapore Permanent Residents and who apply in person at a branch for a personal savings or current account. It does not apply to corporate accounts or to applications made through the mobile app.

## 3 Required Documents
### 3.1 Identity Document
The applicant must present a valid original passport.
### 3.2 Singapore Pass
Applicants residing in Singapore must present a valid Employment Pass, S Pass, Dependant's Pass or Student's Pass with at least 6 months of remaining validity.
### 3.3 Proof of Residential Address
The applicant must provide proof of residential address dated within the last 3 months, such as a utility bill or a tenancy agreement.

## 4 Non-Resident Applicants
Applicants who do not hold a valid Singapore pass are non-resident applicants. A non-resident account requires enhanced due diligence and approval from the Branch Manager before the account is opened.

## 5 Initial Deposit
The minimum initial deposit is $1,000 for non-resident applicants and $500 for applicants holding a valid Singapore pass.

## 6 Politically Exposed Persons
If the applicant is a politically exposed person, the account may only be opened after approval from Group Compliance. Branch staff must not open the account before this approval is received.
