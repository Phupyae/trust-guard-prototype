# Staff Expense Claims Standard Operating Procedure

## 1 Purpose
This standard operating procedure (SOP) describes how employees of Northbridge Bank (a fictional organisation) submit, approve and receive reimbursement for work-related expense claims, including overseas travel claims.

## 2 Roles
### 2.1 Claimant
The claimant is the employee who incurred the expense and submits the claim.
### 2.2 Approver
The approver is the claimant's direct manager, or the Department Head where the policy requires it. An approver may not approve their own claim.
### 2.3 Finance Operations
Finance Operations checks approved claims for completeness and processes the reimbursement payment.

## 3 Claim Procedure
### 3.1 Step 1 Complete the Claim Form
The claimant completes the electronic claim form in the Expense Portal, selecting the correct expense category and cost centre.
### 3.2 Step 2 Attach Receipts
The claimant attaches itemised receipts for each expense above $25. Credit card statements alone are not accepted as receipts.
### 3.3 Step 3 Obtain Approval
The claim is routed automatically to the claimant's direct manager, who must approve or reject it within 5 working days.
### 3.4 Step 4 Finance Processing
Finance Operations processes approved claims within 10 working days of approval.
### 3.5 Step 5 Payment
Reimbursements are paid into the employee's salary account with the next monthly payroll cycle.

## 4 Rejected Claims
A rejected claim may be corrected and resubmitted once, within 14 calendar days of the rejection notice. A claim rejected a second time must be escalated to Finance Operations for review.

## 5 Records Retention
Finance Operations retains claim records and receipts for 7 years for audit purposes.
