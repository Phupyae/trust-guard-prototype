# Business Travel FAQ (Team Intranet Page)

## 1 About This Page
Quick answers to questions our team gets asked a lot about business trips. Written by the Sales Operations team in May 2024 from our own experience. Not an official policy document.

## 2 How much is the daily allowance?
You get $120 a day for meals and getting around while you are overseas.

## 3 Do I need to keep receipts?
Yes. Keep receipts for everything, including meals covered by the daily allowance, otherwise Finance may reject your claim.

## 4 Can I fly business class?
Business class is allowed for long-haul flights over 8 hours.

## 5 How long do I have to submit my claim?
You have about two months after you get back, so there is no rush.

## 6 Can I claim laundry?
Yes, laundry is claimable if your trip is 3 days or longer.

## 7 When will I get my money back?
Finance usually pays within about two weeks of your manager approving the claim.
