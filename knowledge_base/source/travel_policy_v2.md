# Employee Overseas Business Travel Policy

## 1 Purpose
This policy sets out the rules for reimbursing expenses incurred by employees of Northbridge Bank (a fictional organisation) while travelling overseas on approved business. All amounts in this policy are stated in Singapore Dollars (SGD).

## 2 Scope
This policy applies to all permanent and contract employees who travel outside Singapore for business purposes. It does not apply to personal travel, secondments longer than 90 days, or travel paid for by a client.

## 3 Definitions
### 3.1 Business Trip
A business trip is overseas travel that has been approved in advance by the employee's direct manager through the Travel Request Form.
### 3.2 Daily Allowance
The daily allowance is a fixed amount intended to cover meals, local transport and incidental expenses for each full day spent overseas.

## 4 Allowances and Reimbursement
### 4.1 Eligible Expenses
Eligible expenses include airfare, accommodation, the daily allowance, visa fees, and travel insurance not already covered by the Group Travel Insurance plan.
### 4.2 Daily Allowance
Employees may claim up to $150 per day for eligible overseas business travel expenses. All claims require manager approval. Partial days of travel are reimbursed at 50% of the daily allowance.
### 4.3 Accommodation
Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to $250 per night. Stays above this limit require prior written approval from the Department Head.
### 4.4 Air Travel
Economy class must be booked for flights shorter than 8 hours. Business class may be booked for flights of 8 hours or longer, subject to Department Head approval obtained before booking.

## 5 Claim Submission
### 5.1 Submission Deadline
Claims must be submitted through the Expense Portal within 30 calendar days of returning from the trip. Claims submitted after this deadline will be rejected unless the Department Head grants an exception in writing.
### 5.2 Receipts
Original or digital receipts must be attached for every individual expense above $25. The daily allowance does not require receipts.

## 6 Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following expenses will not be reimbursed: alcoholic beverages, personal entertainment, traffic fines, laundry for trips shorter than 5 days, and seat or cabin upgrades that were not approved in advance.

## 7 Approval Requirements
### 7.1 Approval Limits
Claims of up to $2,000 per trip require approval from the employee's direct manager. Claims above $2,000 per trip additionally require approval from the Department Head.
### 7.2 No Automatic Approval
No travel expense claim is approved automatically. Every claim is reviewed by an approver before reimbursement is processed by Finance.
