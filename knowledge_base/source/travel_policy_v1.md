# Employee Overseas Business Travel Policy

## 1 Purpose
This policy sets out the rules for reimbursing expenses incurred by employees of Northbridge Bank (a fictional organisation) while travelling overseas on approved business. All amounts in this policy are stated in Singapore Dollars (SGD).

## 2 Scope
This policy applies to all permanent employees who travel outside Singapore for business purposes.

## 4 Allowances and Reimbursement
### 4.2 Daily Allowance
Employees may claim up to $120 per day for eligible overseas business travel expenses. Claims below $500 are approved automatically by the Expense Portal.
### 4.3 Accommodation
Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to $200 per night.

## 5 Claim Submission
### 5.1 Submission Deadline
Claims must be submitted within 60 calendar days of returning from the trip.
